The Best Thing I've Read Lately

"There Is No Antimemetics Division"

"There is No Antimemetics Division" written by QNTM is a book dealing with a dark science fiction world built around the idea of: how do you deal with things that are harmful to human life that can do harm even through being perceived alone? A dizzying tale dealing with multiple situations about human perception and how it can be manipulated and changed. It takes place across a varying timeline in a dying world, taking full advantage of the fear of the unknown.
